Getting Started

Get up and running with VaultMaster in under a minute.

Installation

VaultMaster is distributed directly via GitHub Releases as a verified Android APK package, giving you complete control over your app installation without third-party store tracking.

1Download the latest APK from GitHub Releases

→ https://github.com/AliAhmedoo5/VaultMaster/releases/latest

2Enable "Install from unknown sources"

On your Android device, open Settings > Security > Install unknown apps (or Apps > Special app access), select your browser or file manager, and toggle on Allow from this source.

3Open the APK file and tap Install

Locate the downloaded `app-release.apk` in your notification tray or Downloads folder and tap it to complete installation.

4Launch VaultMaster and create your account

You can sign in using your standard email address or use Google Sign-In for instant authentication. Once signed in, your offline workspace initializes automatically.

Your First Scan

VaultMaster integrates Google ML Kit Document Scanner natively for real-time edge detection and perspective adjustment.

To capture your first physical document, tap the glowing floating action button (`+`) on the bottom right of the dashboard and select Scan Document. Point your camera at any paper receipt, contract, or ID card. The intelligent scanner will automatically highlight the edges, snap the frame cleanly, correct tilt angles, and allow you to compile multiple pages into a crisp, unified PDF document. Assign it to a category before saving.

Importing Files

Already have digital documents stored across your device? You can consolidate them right into your pocket filing cabinet.

Tap the floating action button and choose Import File. VaultMaster supports importing `PDF`, `DOCX`, `XLSX`, `TXT`, `PNG`, and `JPG` files directly from local storage. Upon ingestion, the app's categorization engine applies semantic color coding (■ Red for PDFs, ■ Blue for Word/Text, ■ Green for Spreadsheets, ■ Orange for Images) so you can rapidly triage files visually.

Setting Up Security Vault

For confidential documents like tax returns, passwords, or personal contracts, VaultMaster offers a dedicated encrypted vault.

Navigate to the Vault tab from the bottom navigation bar. On your first visit, you will be prompted to create a secure 4-digit PIN (or authorize native device biometrics such as FaceID or Fingerprint via OS keychain encryption). To move a document into the vault, tap the three-dot action menu on any file card and select Move to Vault. The high-security vault automatically locks itself the exact moment you background the app, switch tasks, or lock your phone screen.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does VaultMaster work without an internet connection?

Yes! VaultMaster is built with an offline-first architecture. All files, folders, and metadata are stored and indexed locally on your device. When internet connectivity returns, background synchronization seamlessly backs up encrypted assets to Cloudinary and Firebase Firestore.

Where are my documents stored?

Locally, documents reside in private app storage on your Android device. In the cloud, your assets are synchronized to your designated secure storage bucket, accessible only to your authenticated user profile.
